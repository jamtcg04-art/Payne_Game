# Static Hours: Story Bible (v1 draft)

Built on the existing game plan (`01_engine_and_pillars.md`, `04_levels_and_asset_mapping.md`): Port Varro, Dez Harlan, the Greywater Combine, Orla Venn, The Usher, Pia Okafor and Teddy Brandt are kept. Everything new is marked **[GUESS]**. All names, places and beats are invented; nothing is taken from existing games, films or books.

## 1. Tone and pitch

A snowbound harbor city, a disgraced ex-cop with a process server's clipboard, and one dead client whose paperwork leads to the people running the port. Retro-modern: sodium streetlights, payphones, rain-slick alleys, a sparse piano and drone score, graphic-novel panel cutscenes. Dez narrates in a dry, tired voice **[GUESS]**; the approved comic VOX lines ("Mhm", "Bruh") give occasional gallows humor, used sparingly so the noir holds.

Title meaning **[GUESS]**: "static hours" are the dead hours between last call and first shift, when Port Varro's real business happens, and also the radio hiss Dez cannot stop hearing since an old injury.

Themes: being the last honest clerk in a dishonest system, paper as a weapon, debts that follow people.

## 2. Protagonist: Dez Harlan

- 38, former transit-police officer, forced out three years ago after he reported a cargo-theft ring and the file vanished **[GUESS]**. Now a process server: he hands people the worst paper of their lives.
- Look: hoodie or cap and sunglasses variants (UrbanMan body), a worn winter coat, a clipboard that doubles as a prop.
- Flaw: he cannot leave a delivery unfinished. It gets him into trouble and also gets him through it.
- Skills in-fiction: reading paperwork, remembering faces and addresses, calm under pressure, which justifies Hush Time as "the world going quiet" **[GUESS]**.
- Wound **[GUESS]**: his old partner Marta Quill took the fall for the leak he reported and left the city. Her name appears on one page of the ledger.

## 3. Port Varro

A fictional winter harbor city, snow in the hills and sleet at the waterfront. Districts, matched to the levels:
- **Cinder Street** (hub): a plus-shaped intersection of tenements, Pia's stand, Teddy's office, a bus stop and an underpass.
- **Downtown strip**: bars, diners, roadblocks, the financial blocks.
- **Lowline**: rail yards, container stacks, an underpass.
- **The Ledger House**: a records and shipping-insurance office tower, six floors.
- **Harbor Authority docks**: warehouses, cranes, the real seat of power.
- **Eight Floors Down**: flooded service tunnels and pump room under the authority building.

## 4. Factions

1. **Greywater Combine**: dockside smuggling crew that has grown into a logistics business. Thugs, Gunmen and Heavies. Wants the ledger back.
2. **Port Varro Police, Deputy Commissioner Orla Venn's side**: Venn runs a protection arrangement for the Combine and orders roadblocks to catch Dez. Not every officer is dirty; a few beat cops quietly look away **[GUESS]**.
3. **The Usher**: a masked enforcer who works for Venn, not the Combine **[GUESS]**. Cleans up witnesses after the paperwork is done. Seen offscreen in mission 4, boss in mission 6.
4. **Neutral street network**: Pia Okafor's news stand (information, Steadies) and Teddy Brandt's retired-detective office (case files, safe space).

## 5. Plot outline: 6 missions plus a hub

**Hub: Cinder Street.** Mission board, Pia's stand, Teddy's office, one drivable car. Missions unlock by following the paper.

**Opening.** Dez serves papers on a client, Anselm Rook, a shipping clerk **[GUESS]**. Rook is dead on the floor, Dez's prints are on everything, and a ledger page is missing.

1. **Paper Trail (tutorial).** Dez wakes after the delivery goes wrong, is shot at in his own apartment building and flees up a fire escape to the rooftops. Learns move, aim, Hush Time, Throw. He finds a half-burned page: coordinates, ship names, one line signed "M.Q."
2. **Night Shift.** Dez meets Pia, who tells him the ledger belongs to the Harbor Authority. Escapes a police roadblock and fights through a downtown bar where a Combine fixer is meeting a cop. Learns the frame came from inside the police.
3. **Lowline.** Following the shipping page into the rail yard, Dez gets caught in a Combine cargo handoff. Train yard chase, container maze, exploding fuel. Introduces Heavies. He finds Rook's spare key and a location: the Ledger House.
4. **The Ledger House.** Stealth-optional infiltration of the office tower. Dez reaches the vault floor, learns Marta Quill was Rook's contact and that the ledger lists Venn's payments. He sees the Usher at work through a window but cannot stop him. Elevator panel cutscene.
5. **Harbor Authority.** Big docks firefight with crane, warehouses and snipers. Teddy meets Dez with a copy of the original theft report that proves Venn's role. Venn appears in person, offering Dez a quiet way out; he refuses. Combine boss Corwin Hale **[GUESS]** is killed or cornered, a player choice. The only complete ledger is below the Authority building.
6. **Eight Floors Down.** Through flooded tunnels to the pump room. Boss fight with the Usher in three phases (gunfire, lights out, melee). Dez retrieves the ledger; the fight costs Teddy his life and leaves Dez critically injured, and he escapes up to the street at dawn.

**Ending (dark, confirmed by jam):** In the pump room the Usher kills Teddy, who followed Dez down with the original theft report. Dez kills the Usher but is critically injured and drags himself up to the street at dawn with the ledger. Paramedics take him; the ledger is taken by a uniformed officer before he wakes. In the last scene, Dez lies in a hospital bed while Orla Venn, still deputy commissioner, visits in person, thanks him politely for his service and reads a prepared statement closing the case on Rook's murder. She leaves. The radio static rises over a still panel of Dez's monitor. Pia's stand is shuttered; she is the only one who still visits.

**Orla Venn (jam: unseen as a final target until a sequel, but she appears in this game):** she is never fought or brought down in this game. She appears in person or on screen at set moments: a TV press statement in the hub (mission 1), a roadblock radio voice (mission 2), a face-to-face scene at the Harbor Authority (mission 5) where she offers Dez a quiet way out, and the hospital finale. The sequel makes her the final target.

**Marta Quill (left open, confirmed by jam):** her fate is never resolved. Evidence points both ways: her initials on a payment page, a voicemail with only static on it, and a train ticket out of the city. The game never says whether she betrayed Dez, fled, or died. The Usher is her former colleague, not Marta herself.

## 6. Supporting cast

- **Pia Okafor**, night-shift news stand owner, informant, sells Steadies. Warm, sharp, never fooled.
- **Teodor "Teddy" Brandt**, retired detective, bad knees, good files. The father figure Dez never had.
- **Orla Venn**, deputy commissioner. Soft-spoken, reads from prepared statements even in private.
- **The Usher**, masked enforcer. Almost silent; announces himself with a quiet cough **[GUESS]**.
- **Corwin Hale** **[GUESS]**, Combine boss, mid-story antagonist. Dock-bred, proud of his union card.
- **Anselm Rook**, the dead client. Appears in tape and paper fragments.
- **Marta Quill** **[GUESS]**, Dez's old partner, a ghost in the paperwork.

## 7. Motifs for design and audio

- Paper as UI: ledger pages, case board and subpoenas are collectible story items.
- Sodium orange against snow blue; rain and sleet intensify with danger.
- Radio static and low-pass filter on Hush Time, matching the audio plan.
- Graphic-novel still panels between missions, with Dez's voice-over; text captions are always on as a fallback.
- Humor stays in Dez's voice and the stray comic barks; the villains are never funny.
